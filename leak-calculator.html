How this is calculated

We assume the best a clinic can do is turn 20% of every enquiry into a patient who attends. Spam, price-shoppers, wrong-treatment messages and duplicates live in the other 80%, and are never counted against you.

Your answers set three rates: how many enquiries get a reply, how many of those book, and how many booked patients turn up. Multiplied together, they predict your monthly patients. If you entered your real number, that number wins — the model never argues with your books.

Against that we run the same funnel at best-case rates, which multiply to exactly that 20%:

The difference is your leak, split across the three stages in proportion to how far each of your answers sits below the benchmark. The headline counts one treatment per patient. Repeat treatments are shown separately, because the honest number matters more than the big one.

The 20% ceiling and the stage rates are industry-typical figures, not a published UAE study. Treat the result as a well-argued estimate, not an audit.
